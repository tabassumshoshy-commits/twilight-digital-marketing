How It Works

A simple four-step process, so you always know what is happening.

  1. 1

    Free Audit

    You tell me about your business and share your website or social links. I look at your current online presence and find what can be improved.

  2. 2

    Plan & Quote

    You get a plain-language plan: what I will do, how long it will take, and what it will cost. You decide — no pressure, no obligation.

  3. 3

    Do the Work

    I carry out the agreed work — optimisation, content, ads or a new website — and keep you updated as it progresses.

  4. 4

    Report & Improve

    You get clear updates on what changed and what happened next, and we adjust the plan based on real results.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do your services cost?

Every business is different, so I give a custom quote after a free audit. Tell me about your business and your goals, and I will reply with a plan and a price. There is no obligation to go ahead.

What is included in the free audit?

I look at your website and social media presence, note what is working and what can be improved, and send you a short list of recommendations plus a custom quote. It is free, and you are not obliged to hire me afterwards.

How long before I see results?

Honestly: SEO and organic growth take time — usually several months of steady work before clear movement. Paid ads can bring traffic faster. No one can guarantee rankings or sales, and I will not promise what cannot be promised.

Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?

Yes. All work is done online, so your location does not matter. Communication is by email and messaging.

Can you write the content too?

Yes — content and blogging is one of the services. I write blog posts, articles and website copy in clear, simple English, formatted for readers and search engines.

What do you need from me to start?

Your website or social media links, a short description of your business, and your goals. The free audit form below covers all of it.
